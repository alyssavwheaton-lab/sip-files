More About the SIP Suite

The SIP Suite is a coordinated framework — three strategies that share the same investment process, team, and philosophy. Rather than selecting individual income products independently, advisors can use the suite to match clients to the strategy that fits their income posture and risk profile, all within a consistent approach.

The three strategies map to distinct client profiles: SIP serves as the income anchor for clients prioritizing capital discipline and steady income. SIP Plus targets clients seeking higher income potential who are willing to accept measured credit risk. Global SIP broadens income sources for portfolios that need geographic diversification beyond U.S. markets.

Eagle's income investment team actively manages allocation across equities, fixed income, and cash within defined ranges (Stocks 35-65%, Bonds 35-65%, Cash 0-30%). Tactical adjustments are made as market conditions evolve, positioning the portfolio proactively rather than reactively.

Eagle's income team publishes a monthly video briefing with current allocation positioning and actionable takeaways, hosts quarterly live webinars with Q&A, and maintains the Amplify Income Hub with strategy factsheets, the Income Playbook, and practice-ready tools — all updated regularly.
