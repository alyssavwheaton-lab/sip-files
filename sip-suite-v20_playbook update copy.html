Practice Management

Built for the Income Conversation Your Client is Already Having

When a client says “I want more yield,” most advisors have one answer. The SIP Suite gives you three — each calibrated to a different risk appetite and life stage, all backed by the same institutional process.

The Retiree Who Needs Income Now SIP anchors the conversation — capital discipline, consistent income, institutional-grade oversight.
The Client Who Wants More Yield SIP Plus steps up income potential through higher-yielding credit and equities — with the same Eagle oversight framework.
The Client Who Wants Global Diversification Global SIP opens international income sources — broadening yield beyond U.S. markets without abandoning the discipline.
