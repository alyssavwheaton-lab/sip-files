The Framework

Your clients’ income needs will change. Your framework should not break when they do.

Position the SIP Suite as a practice framework, not three separate products. The point is not just selection. It is continuity across the client lifecycle.

A single suite built to support movement across income priorities.

Advisors can bring one income process into the relationship and then calibrate how that process is expressed as client needs evolve, whether the conversation centers on capital preservation, a step-up in income potential, or broader global opportunity.

The Anchor

Strategic Income Portfolio (SIP)

Core Income | Conservative–Moderate

Designed as the foundational expression of the suite, SIP emphasizes risk-aware income generation, active multi-asset allocation, and a structure that can anchor income discussions in more defensive client situations.

Client fit: Advisors positioning a more measured income solution for clients who prioritize stability, downside awareness, and disciplined portfolio construction.
Risk profile: Lower Income role: Core Use case: Anchor

Use SIP when the client conversation begins with preserving the integrity of accumulated capital while maintaining a disciplined path to income through active stock, bond, and cash management.

The Step-Up

Strategic Income Portfolio Plus

Increased Income | Moderate

SIP Plus is intended for clients where the income conversation can extend further up the risk spectrum. It preserves the suite’s active, multi-asset discipline while seeking a stronger income expression.

Client fit: Advisors looking to step up income potential for clients comfortable with a somewhat higher-risk profile but still wanting a disciplined, balanced process.
Risk profile: Moderate Income role: Elevated Use case: Step-up

The Broader Reach

Global Income | Moderate

Global SIP extends the same suite logic into a broader opportunity set, helping advisors position income conversations where global diversification and an expanded market lens are part of the client objective.
