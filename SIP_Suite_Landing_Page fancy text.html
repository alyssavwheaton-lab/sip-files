The Investment Approach

A Fine-Tuned
Approach to Income

Eagle's SIP Suite was built in the wake of the financial crisis — forged through volatility, refined across full market cycles. The result is a unified framework that prioritizes disciplined risk management, active tactical positioning, and an unwavering focus on generating consistent, durable income for clients regardless of where markets move.

"One process. Three strategies. Every income conversation covered — designed to support client needs across varying risk profiles and objectives."

James Camp, CFA — Managing Director, Strategic Income
01
Capital Preservation
Disciplined risk management across equities, bonds, and cash — the foundation of every SIP strategy. Protecting principal is not secondary to income; it is integral to it.
02
Steady Income Production
Consistently producing income through dividend-paying equities and fixed income securities. Each strategy in the suite is calibrated to a distinct yield and risk objective.
03
Broad Market Exposure
Both equity and bond exposure with allocation flexibility to respond to shifting market conditions — providing advisors with a dynamic tool that adapts alongside their clients' needs.
04
Active Tactical Management
Allocation decisions driven by a proprietary model incorporating the Fed model, real dividend growth rates, economic liquidity, stock/bond trend data, trader sentiment, and interest-rate expectations — not a static mandate. Tenured teams with the autonomy to act within a unified, risk-conscious framework refined across full market cycles.
The Product Suite

Match the Right Solution
to Every Client Profile

Each strategy in the SIP Suite addresses a distinct income objective — from conservative capital preservation to enhanced yield to global diversification. One unified process. Three ways to meet clients where they are.

Strategy selection should be based on client income objective, risk tolerance, time horizon, and investment minimums. Consult your Eagle RVP for allocation guidance.

SIP — The Anchor
Strategic Income Portfolio
Steady Income & Consistent Growth
Eagle's flagship income strategy combining U.S. dividend-paying equities and investment grade fixed income. Built for clients seeking reliable income with equity upside participation and a disciplined capital preservation mandate.
Approach Dividend growth equities + investment grade bonds. Dynamic 35–65% stock/bond allocation.
Benchmark 50% S&P 500 / 50% Bloomberg Intermediate Govt/Credit
Best For Clients seeking stable income with moderate growth potential

SIP+ — The Step-Up
Strategic Income Portfolio Plus
Increased Income & Steady Growth
An extension of the core SIP process with enhanced yield through BBB/BB corporate credit and higher-dividend equities. Includes a tax-free municipal bond option — designed for clients who require greater income generation while maintaining a risk-aware framework.
Approach Higher-dividend equities + BBB/BB corporate credit + municipal bond option (tax-free sleeve)
Benchmark 50% Dow Jones Dividend Select / 25% Bloomberg Baa Corp / 25% Bloomberg Ba Corp
Best For Income-oriented clients with tolerance for moderate credit risk; taxable and tax-advantaged accounts
