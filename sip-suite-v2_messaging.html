Advisor Positioning

Three Ways to Position the SIP Suite

Verbatim scripts for your next client conversation. Select and copy the one that fits.

SIP “I want income. I can’t lose principal.”

The client who wants reliable income and won’t accept principal risk — that’s SIP. Dividend equities plus investment-grade fixed income, 35 to 65 in either direction, 50-to-70 names. The team adjusts the mix based on the Fed model and real dividend growth data. The client gets consistent income; you get a defensible allocation.

Request SIP materials
SIP Plus “I need more yield. Watch the risk.”

The client who needs more yield but still wants oversight on the risk — that’s SIP Plus. Same framework as SIP, but the fixed income sleeve adds selective BBB/BB-rated credit with a 50/50 target split and max 15% BB overweight. There’s also a municipal bond option for tax-sensitive clients. Quality-first, not yield-chasing.

Request SIP Plus materials
Global SIP “Income beyond U.S. markets.”

The client who wants income but also wants exposure beyond U.S. markets — that’s Global SIP. ClariVest runs the international dividend equity sleeve; Eagle runs domestic equity income and investment-grade bonds. One philosophy, a broader opportunity set, a single client conversation.
