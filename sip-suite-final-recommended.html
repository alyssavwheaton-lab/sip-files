Suite Overview

One suite. Three ways to approach income.

The Strategic Income Portfolio Suite is designed to help advisors align income solutions more precisely to client objectives, risk tolerance, and market opportunity. Each strategy serves a distinct role. Each is actively managed. Each reflects the same disciplined investment philosophy from Eagle Asset Management.

Rather than forcing every client into the same income answer, the suite gives advisors a more complete toolkit for matching the portfolio to the conversation in front of them. It is a cleaner way to move from market backdrop to client need to strategy recommendation.

A disciplined process applied across the full suite
Three distinct approaches to income and risk
Built for different client priorities, not one-size-fits-all allocation decisions
Backed by Eagle's active management perspective
Why this matters now

Clients are asking sharper questions about yield, downside resilience, diversification, and portfolio role. A suite approach helps advisors answer those questions with more precision and more control over how risk is introduced into the conversation.

How to position it

Lead with the client objective. Then show how the suite can scale from steadier income, to higher-income potential, to broader global diversification without abandoning a consistent investment discipline.

Advisor Client Fit

Start with the client conversation.

The strongest income discussion does not begin with product features. It begins with what the client needs the portfolio to do. Select a conversation path below to surface the strategy most aligned to that objective.

Steadier income anchor

For clients seeking a steadier core allocation

They want income potential with an emphasis on balance, diversification, and a disciplined approach to risk.

Higher-income step-up

For clients willing to accept more credit risk for higher income potential

They are open to a more opportunistic income posture, provided the risk is being actively managed and intentionally sized.

Broader opportunity set

For clients open to expanding beyond U.S. markets for income diversification

They want income potential supported by a broader opportunity set and may benefit from global dividend exposure alongside fixed income.

Strategic Income Portfolio

SIP

Core income anchor

A balanced approach for clients seeking a steadier source of income with active management across a diversified mix of income-producing assets.

  • Steady income potential and balanced growth
  • Useful starting point for a core income conversation
  • Designed for clients who want income without reaching too far out on the risk spectrum
Advisor framing

A disciplined starting point for clients who want income and balance in the same conversation.

Strategic Income Portfolio Plus

SIP Plus

Higher-income extension

Designed for clients seeking increased income potential through a more credit-oriented posture, while still benefiting from a disciplined and actively managed process.

  • Greater income potential with additional credit exposure
  • Supports a step-up yield discussion
  • Best for clients comfortable with a more opportunistic income profile
Advisor framing

A step-up income conversation for clients willing to accept additional credit risk in exchange for higher income potential.

Global Strategic Income Portfolio

Global SIP

Global income diversifier

Designed for clients who may benefit from a broader global opportunity set, combining dividend-oriented equities and fixed income to support a differentiated income conversation.

  • Income potential with broader geographic diversification
  • Supports a more global conversation around income sourcing
  • Useful when the advisor discussion expands beyond U.S.-only solutions
Advisor framing

A useful option when the conversation shifts toward broader diversification and a wider global income opportunity set.
