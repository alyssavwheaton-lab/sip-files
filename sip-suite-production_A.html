Eagle Asset Management, a boutique in the Raymond James Investment Management family, provides a broad array of fundamental equity and fixed-income strategies designed to meet the long-term goals of institutional and individual investors. Eagle's multiple independent investment teams have the autonomy to pursue investment decisions guided by their individual philosophies and strategies.

The information provided should not be construed as a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any particular strategy or security. The data is shown for informational purposes only and is not indicative of future portfolio characteristics or returns. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Equity Income investing involves risks, including that dividend-issuing companies may choose not to pay a dividend or may not have the ability to pay. Fixed income investing involves risk, including interest-rate risk, credit risk and reinvestment risk. Strategies that invest in below-investment-grade ("high-yield") securities involve greater credit and liquidity risk. International investments involve additional risks, including currency fluctuations, political and economic instability, and differences in accounting and disclosure standards. All investments involve risk, including possible loss of principal. Diversification and asset allocation do not ensure a profit or protect against loss.
