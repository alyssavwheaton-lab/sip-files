The framework

Your clients’ income needs will change. Your framework shouldn’t break when they do.

The SIP Suite is designed as a practice framework, not three disconnected products. Advisors can keep the conversation, the team and the process intact while matching the income approach more precisely to client objectives, risk tolerance and market conditions.

Life changes. Markets change. The suite gives advisors room to move within one disciplined process rather than starting over with every new client need.
Three strategies

Match the income approach to the client conversation.

Each strategy sits inside the same quality-first framework, but with a different risk-and-income emphasis. The goal is simple: faster fit, clearer positioning, better follow-through in the meeting.

“I want reliable income and I don’t want to worry about my principal.”
The Anchor

Strategic Income Portfolio (SIP)

Core income | Conservative–moderate

SIP is the foundation of the suite, blending dividend-paying equities with investment-grade fixed income in a dynamic, risk-aware portfolio. It is built for advisors who want steady income potential and a disciplined path toward consistent growth.

Client fit Retirees, conservative balanced investors and clients prioritizing capital discipline alongside income.
Income: steady Risk: lower Blend: dividend equity + IG fixed income
Lead with SIP when the conversation starts with dependable income, quality exposure and a preference for a more conservative multi-asset posture. It is the cleanest entry point for clients who want the suite philosophy without stepping up the credit or global allocation dial.
Allocation range35–65% stocks / bonds
ObjectiveSteady income + growth
ProcessDynamic macro-informed mix

The Step-Up

Strategic Income Portfolio Plus

Higher income potential | Moderate

SIP Plus is the higher-yielding expression within the suite, pairing dividend-focused equities with carefully constructed BBB and BB corporate credit exposure. It is designed for investors seeking more income without giving up active oversight or portfolio discipline.

Client fit Yield-seeking investors with moderate risk tolerance who want more income potential than a typical balanced solution.
Income: higher Risk: moderate Blend: dividend equity + BBB/BB credit
Lead with SIP Plus when the client objective is to elevate income potential while staying inside a risk-aware, actively managed framework. The step-up comes from the credit profile and yield mix, not from abandoning discipline.
Allocation range35–65% equities / fixed income
Credit profileBalanced BBB / BB focus
ObjectiveIncreased income + growth

The Diversifier

Global Strategic Income Portfolio

Global diversification | Moderate

Global SIP extends the suite’s balanced allocation approach beyond domestic markets, combining U.S. dividend equities, international dividend exposure and investment-grade fixed income. It is built for clients who want diversified income potential with a global reach.

Client fit Investors seeking broader geographic diversification while retaining a risk-aware income framework.
Income: diversified Risk: moderate Blend: U.S. + international dividend equity
Lead with Global SIP when the income conversation includes diversification by region, yield source or market opportunity set. It keeps the suite’s quality-first process intact while broadening where income can come from.
Long-term mixApprox. 50/50 stocks / bonds
Equity sleevesU.S. + international dividend
ObjectiveGlobal income + appreciation

More information

Give the advisor a little more depth without making the page heavy.

The suite operates as one investment framework with three distinct expressions. That creates more flexibility at the client-fit level without forcing the advisor to reintroduce an entirely different philosophy every time the objective changes.
Lead with SIP when capital discipline and dependable income are the priority. Step into SIP Plus when the objective shifts toward higher income potential. Use Global SIP when diversification by region and broader yield sources becomes part of the conversation.
Position the suite as a conversation framework rather than a product menu. Start with the client need, then show how the range of strategies allows the advisor to stay inside one disciplined process while adjusting the income posture.
The support team can provide the suite overview piece, individual factsheets, side-by-side strategy comparisons, positioning guidance for advisor meetings and follow-up help after the first conversation.
