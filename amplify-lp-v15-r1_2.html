Common Questions

What advisors ask before recommending the SIP Suite

The SIP Suite is actively managed with tactical allocation shifts within defined ranges. Unlike static models, the team adjusts positioning based on their market outlook. And unlike a single balanced strategy, the suite offers three distinct risk/yield profiles so you can match each client's income objective without leaving the framework.
Eagle selected ClariVest Asset Management to bring institutional-caliber international equity selection to the Global SIP framework. ClariVest manages the international dividend equity sleeve, while Eagle manages the domestic equity income and fixed income components. Eagle is the architect; ClariVest is the specialist they chose.
SIP and Global SIP each require a $250,000 minimum. SIP Plus requires $250,000 for Tax-Free accounts and $500,000 for Tax-Aware. SIP Plus uniquely includes a municipal bond sleeve option, making it the only suite strategy with a dedicated tax-free fixed income component.
Start with the client conversation: SIP (The Anchor) suits clients who prioritize income stability. SIP Plus (The Step-Up) is for clients willing to accept higher volatility for more yield. Global SIP (The Diversifier) fits clients who want geographic diversification alongside income. Your Market Leader can help walk through specific scenarios.
Rebalancing is tactical and ongoing — not calendar-driven. The team adjusts allocations based on changing macro conditions, interest rate expectations, credit spreads, equity valuations, and client income objectives. Each month, the team shares their current positioning rationale through the Strategic Income Playbook video series, so you always know where the portfolio stands and why.
