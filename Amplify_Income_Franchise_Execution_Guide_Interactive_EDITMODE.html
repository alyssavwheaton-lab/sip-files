02 Section

1. Strategic Reframe: From Campaign to Income Franchise

This document supersedes the original SIP Suite Campaign Playbook timeline and integrates all campaign elements under a unified income franchise strategy. The revised approach elevates Eagle’s income positioning from a product-level campaign to a durable content franchise modeled on the most effective distribution marketing programs in institutional asset management.

Notes
03 Subsection

1.1 The Franchise Model

The highest-performing income marketing programs in the industry — PIMCO’s Income Strategy platform, JPMorgan’s Guide to the Markets, Capital Group’s Practice Lab — share a common architecture. They establish the firm as an ongoing income resource first, then position products within that ecosystem. The income narrative is permanent infrastructure; product introductions are activations within it.

Eagle’s “Amplify Your Income Potential” should operate identically. The brand is not a campaign tagline for the SIP Suite launch. It is Eagle’s income franchise umbrella under which every income-related touchpoint lives: the quarterly webinar, the monthly video series, the Income Playbook chart book, the Client Convos quarterly, the Income Toolkit, and the SIP Suite product marketing. Each component reinforces the others. No single asset carries the entire weight of the income story.

Notes
04 Subsection

1.2 Content Hierarchy

The franchise operates across four tiers, each at a different cadence, serving a different function in the advisor relationship lifecycle. The tiers are designed so that content flows downhill — quarterly anchor content feeds the monthly series, which feeds the campaign activations, which feed the sustained engagement tools.

TIERCADENCEASSETSFUNCTION
1. Quarterly Anchor4x/yearIncome Webinar + Income Playbook (chart book)Tentpole event. Establishes market authority. Produces source material for all other tiers.
2. Monthly Pulse12x/yearStrategic Income Playbook Video SeriesRecurring engagement. Keeps Eagle visible between tentpoles. Delivers utility to advisors.
3. Campaign ActivationsAs scheduledSIP Suite Email 1, SIP+ Email 2, GSIP Email 3, webinar promo emailsDirect product introductions. Runs within the franchise ecosystem, not outside it.
4. Sustained ToolsOngoingClient Convos, Income Toolkit/Briefcase, Social clips, Seismic DSRsKeeps franchise alive between active campaigns. Physical and digital artifacts.

The critical design principle: Tier 1 and Tier 2 content is positioned as market intelligence and advisor utility, not product promotion. The product case is implicit — the team producing the content is the team managing the strategies. Tier 3 is the only tier that makes an explicit product ask. This ensures the franchise maintains credibility across the full advisor audience, including those who have not yet allocated.

05 Section

2. Video Series: The Recurring Content Engine

The monthly allocation video series is the most strategically important recurring asset in the franchise. It operates at Tier 2 — monthly cadence, market intelligence positioning, advisor utility focus. It is the single touchpoint that keeps Eagle’s income team visible to advisors every 30 days without requiring a product-level ask.

Notes
06 Subsection

2.1 Franchise Positioning (Not Product Positioning)

The video series should be branded under the Amplify franchise, not under the SIP Suite product brand. The recommended series title is “Strategic Income Playbook” or “Amplify Income Monthly.” The content structure remains unchanged from the Supplemental Execution Guide:

Opening (30 sec): Market context — what happened, what it means for income investors.

Portfolio Positioning (60–90 sec): Current allocation stance, tactical shifts, and rationale.

Income Lens (60 sec): Yield, credit, or dividend trends relevant to income strategies.

Advisor Takeaway (30 sec): One actionable insight for the next client conversation.

Close (15 sec): Standard CTA to cloud page or upcoming webinar.

Target length: 3–4 minutes. Format: talking head with supporting charts. The Advisor Takeaway is the most valuable segment — it is the clip that gets shared on social, referenced in RVP emails, and used by advisors in practice. Every video must deliver one specific, usable takeaway.

Notes
07 Subsection

2.2 How the Video Series Integrates With Every Touchpoint

Cloud Page

The latest monthly video occupies the primary content position on the cloud page, above the evergreen Camp suite overview video. The cloud page should feature a “Latest Episode” badge on the current month’s video and an archive slider or list of previous episodes below. This transforms the cloud page from a static product hub into a living franchise destination that advisors have a reason to revisit monthly.

Sales Email Templates (RVP Monthly Playbook)

The RVP playbook email leads with the monthly video, includes 2–3 key takeaways formatted as conversation starters, and closes with the next upcoming touchpoint (webinar registration or next video teaser). Template structure: personal note from the RVP (1–2 sentences), video link with speaker and topic, key takeaways, secondary CTA. The RJ version references the SIP Suite availability. The Non-RJ version frames the content as Eagle’s income market intelligence without product-specific language.

Marketing Campaign Emails

Email 1 (SIP Suite introduction) references the video series as an ongoing resource within the Amplify ecosystem. Emails 2 and 3 (SIP+, GSIP) incorporate relevant clips or references from the most recent monthly video that address strategy-specific themes — higher-yield positioning for SIP+, international allocation for GSIP. The monthly videos become embedded proof points within the campaign emails.

Quarterly Income Webinar

The webinar is positioned as the “live, extended version” of the monthly playbook. Same investment team, deeper format, live Q&A. The monthly videos build familiarity with the speakers and content approach; the webinar capitalizes on that familiarity to drive live engagement. Post-webinar, the replay becomes an archive asset alongside the monthly videos on the cloud page.

Social Media

Each monthly video produces 2–3 short clips (30–60 seconds) for LinkedIn, focused on the Advisor Takeaway segment. Posts are branded under the Amplify franchise and link to the full episode on the cloud page. Social cadence: publish clips within 48 hours of video going live, then a second promotional push 5–7 days later for extended reach.

Seismic DSR

Each monthly video plus its key takeaways document is packaged as a DSR that the sales team can send 1:1. The DSR includes the video link, the takeaways formatted as bullet points, and a pre-written email body the salesperson can customize. The DSR updates monthly as part of the standard publish workflow.

Income Playbook Chart Book

The quarterly Income Playbook (modeled on JPMorgan’s Guide to the Markets) draws from the same source material as the monthly videos. The webinar deck provides the raw charts; the monthly videos provide the narrative context. The Playbook is the printed and digital artifact that synthesizes a quarter’s worth of income market intelligence into a reference document advisors can keep on their desk and use in client meetings.

Income Toolkit / Briefcase

The physical Income Toolkit includes a QR code linking to the cloud page video archive. When an advisor scans the QR code from a conference or desk drop, they land on a page populated with months of video content, the latest Income Playbook, webinar replays, and strategy factsheets. The Toolkit bridges physical distribution and digital engagement.

12 Subsection

4.1 Quarterly Income Webinar

The quarterly webinar is the Tier 1 tentpole event that anchors the entire franchise calendar. Each quarter, the webinar delivers a comprehensive income market update from the full investment team, covering macro conditions, allocation positioning, yield and credit analysis, and forward-looking strategy.

The March webinar (targeting last week of March) should be positioned as the Q1 2026 Amplify Your Income Potential Quarterly Update. It is a market intelligence event, not a product sales event. The team that manages the SIP Suite delivers the content, which establishes product familiarity implicitly without requiring an explicit sell. Advisors register for market intelligence. They attend for investment insight. They leave with awareness of the SIP Suite as the implementation vehicle.

Webinar-to-Playbook Pipeline

Within two weeks of the live event, the webinar deck content is repurposed into the quarterly Income Playbook chart book. The Playbook extracts the most impactful charts and data visualizations from the webinar, formats them as standalone reference pages (each page tells a complete story without narration), and adds 2–3 pages of SIP Suite positioning within the current market context. The Playbook is the artifact that outlasts the webinar — it sits on advisors’ desks, gets shared with colleagues, and resurfaces in client meetings for the next 90 days.

Webinar Promotion Integration

Webinar promotion leverages every tier of the franchise. The monthly video series builds familiarity with the speakers (“You’ve seen the monthly playbook. Now join the full team live.”). The RVP combo email template pairs the latest monthly video with the webinar registration link. Social posts clip content from recent monthly videos with a CTA to register for the deeper live session. RJnet banners drive awareness among the internal channel. This multi-touch promotion approach ensures the webinar benefits from months of franchise engagement rather than starting from zero awareness with each quarterly event.

13 Subsection

4.2 Income Playbook (JPMorgan Guide to the Markets Model)

The Income Playbook is Eagle’s version of JPMorgan’s Guide to the Markets — a quarterly, chart-driven reference document that advisors use as a practice tool. It is not a brochure. It is not a sales piece. It is a market reference that happens to be produced by the team that manages the SIP Suite. This positioning distinction is essential to the Playbook’s utility and longevity.

Content Architecture

Each edition: 15–20 pages. The content is drawn directly from Camp’s quarterly income webinar deck, curated and reformatted into a chart book layout where every page tells a self-contained story. Sections include macro environment and rate outlook, yield curve and credit market analysis, equity income and dividend trend data, asset allocation positioning across the SIP Suite strategies, and historical income performance context. The final 2–3 pages position the SIP Suite within the current market environment — this is the only section with explicit product reference.

Production Cadence

Draft assembled from webinar deck 1–2 weeks post-webinar. Design production: 1 week for chart formatting and Amplify branding. Compliance review: 1 week. Target distribution: 3–4 weeks post-webinar. Distribution channels: Cloud Page (downloadable PDF), RJnet, Seismic, and physical copies for RVPs to carry and leave behind.

First Edition Target

The Q1 2026 Income Playbook first edition is assembled from the March webinar materials. Production begins the first week of April, targeting distribution by the end of April. This aligns with Email 2 (SIP+ introduction), creating a natural package: the advisor receives the SIP+ email, clicks through to the cloud page, and finds the Income Playbook as a complementary resource. The Playbook validates Eagle’s income authority; the email makes the specific product case.

14 Subsection

4.3 Client Convos Quarterly

Client Convos is the client-facing companion piece that translates institutional-quality market intelligence into advisor-ready conversation frameworks. Where the Income Playbook is a reference tool for the advisor’s own use, Client Convos is designed for the advisor-client interaction. It bridges the gap between what the investment team knows and what the advisor says in the next meeting.

Content Framework

Each quarterly edition features 3–4 conversation starters drawn from the webinar and current market themes. Each conversation starter includes a plain-language client explanation (no jargon), a supporting data point or chart, a recommended positioning statement the advisor can use, and a connection to the relevant SIP Suite strategy (SIP, SIP+, or GSIP). The tone mirrors best-in-class client communication: clear, confident, and empathetic to client concerns about income, risk, and market uncertainty.

Pilot Edition Timeline

Workshop the first edition with the sales team in late April to validate format and content. The pilot draws from the March webinar themes. Feedback loop: sales input determines whether the cadence is quarterly or semi-annual, and whether the format is digital-only or includes a physical print run for RVP distribution.

4.4 Income Toolkit / Briefcase

The Income Toolkit is the physical manifestation of the Amplify franchise. Repurposed from the SIMS briefcase framework, it provides external wholesalers with a curated, portable collection of Eagle’s income materials that can be left behind at advisor meetings, used at conferences, and shipped as desk drops.

Toolkit Contents

Each Toolkit includes the three SIP Suite strategy factsheets (SIP, SIP+, Global SIP), a condensed version of the latest Income Playbook (4–6 pages), a one-page “Which Strategy Fits Your Client?” comparison card, and a QR code card linking to the cloud page video archive and full resources. The QR code is the bridge between the physical and digital ecosystems — it converts a conference interaction into an ongoing digital engagement.

Distribution Strategy

The Toolkit is deployed alongside the Bluetooth speaker desk drops to the top 100–200 advisor targets. The speaker is the attention-getter. The Toolkit is the substance. Each package includes a handwritten note from the RVP referencing the latest monthly video or the upcoming webinar, creating a personal connection that the physical materials reinforce. Target availability: Q2 2026, with the first print run commissioned after the Playbook first edition is finalized.

6. Sales Enablement and RVP Activation

The sales enablement strategy treats the video series as the primary recurring touchpoint between the RVP and the advisor. Every monthly video produces a ready-made reason for the RVP to reach out. The key templates, email cadences, and Seismic DSR packaging are designed so the sales team can activate within 24 hours of each video going live.

Notes
21 Subsection

6.1 Sales Email Template Suite

Four email templates serve different scenarios across the monthly cycle. All templates are maintained in Salesforce and updated with each new video episode.

Template 1 (RJ — Monthly Playbook): Leads with the video, includes key takeaways, references SIP Suite availability, and includes a P.S. for the next webinar. Used by RVPs for their RJ advisor relationships.

Template 2 (Non-RJ — Monthly Playbook): Same structure but frames content as Eagle’s income market intelligence without SIP Suite product language. Direct Kaltura video links. Used for external channel relationships.

Template 3 (Re-engagement): Shorter, more direct. Targets non-openers from the prior month’s playbook email. Leads with one specific, compelling detail from the video to drive curiosity.

Template 4 (Combo — Playbook + Webinar): Pairs the monthly video with the upcoming webinar registration link. Used during the 2–3 weeks leading up to each quarterly webinar. Positions the video as the appetizer and the webinar as the main course.

Notes
22 Subsection

6.2 Seismic DSR Packaging

Each monthly video generates a Seismic Digital Sales Room update that includes the video link (embedded or direct Kaltura URL), the key takeaways document (2–3 talking points formatted for phone or email use), and a pre-written email body the salesperson can copy and customize. The DSR also includes the latest factsheets and the current quarter’s Income Playbook once available. DSR updates follow the same 3rd Thursday cadence as the video publish.

3rd Thursday (publish day): Video goes live across all platforms. Seismic DSR updated. SF email templates refreshed. Social post published.

8. Measurement Framework

The franchise model shifts measurement from campaign-level metrics to franchise engagement depth. Traditional campaign metrics (open rates, click-through rates, conversion on a single email) remain relevant for Tier 3 activations but are insufficient to measure the health of the broader income franchise.

Notes
29 Subsection

8.1 Franchise Health Metrics

Monthly video view rate: Kaltura analytics on each episode. Target: month-over-month growth in unique viewers as the series builds awareness. Track completion rate to validate the 3–4 minute format.

Cloud page return visits: Percentage of visitors who return to the cloud page within 30 days. This is the key indicator that the franchise is creating a destination, not just a one-time landing experience.

RVP playbook email engagement: Open and click rates on the monthly sales email templates. This measures whether the sales team is actively using the franchise assets and whether advisors are engaging with the 1:1 distribution channel.

Webinar registration and attendance: Registration volume, attendance rate, and replay views. Track the percentage of webinar registrants who previously engaged with the monthly video series to validate the content ladder hypothesis.

Seismic DSR sends and views: Volume of DSR sends per month and advisor engagement with DSR content. This measures sales team adoption of the franchise assets for 1:1 outreach.

Notes
30 Subsection

8.2 Campaign Activation Metrics

Standard campaign metrics for Tier 3 emails: open rate, click-through rate, click-to-open rate, unsubscribe rate, and downstream conversion (factsheet downloads, cloud page visits, form submissions). Segment reporting by audience: current clients vs. prospects, Email 1 openers vs. non-openers for Emails 2 and 3.
