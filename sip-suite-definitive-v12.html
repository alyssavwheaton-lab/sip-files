Strategic Income Portfolio

Steady income and consistent growth

Dividend-paying equities and investment-grade fixed income in one actively managed framework. The team uses the Fed model, real dividend growth rates, economic liquidity, and sentiment data to determine the optimal stock-bond allocation within defined ranges.

SIP Plus

Higher-yielding income with disciplined credit focus

Targets higher income through a blend of high-dividend equities and carefully constructed BBB/BB-rated corporate credit. Within fixed income, targets a balanced 50/50 split of BBB and BB bonds with a maximum 15% BB overweight. Includes a tax-free municipal bond sleeve option.

Diversified, international income

Three Ways to Position the SIP Suite

1

The client who says “I want reliable income and I don’t want to worry about my principal” — that’s SIP. Dividend equities plus investment-grade fixed income, 35 to 65 in either direction, 50-to-70 names. The team adjusts the mix; the client gets the income.

2

The client who says “I want more yield but I still want someone watching the risk” — that’s SIP Plus. Same framework, but the fixed income sleeve adds selective BBB/BB-rated credit with a 50/50 target split and max 15% BB overweight. Plus a municipal bond option for tax-sensitive clients. Quality-first, not yield-chasing.

3

The client who says “I want income, but I also want exposure beyond U.S. markets” — that’s Global SIP. ClariVest runs the international dividend equity sleeve; Eagle runs the domestic equity income and investment-grade bonds. One philosophy, broader opportunity set.
