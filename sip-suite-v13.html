Three Ways to Position
the SIP Suite

Verbatim positioning language from your Regional VP team — match it to the client conversation in front of you.

01
SIP — The Anchor

“I want reliable income and I don’t want to worry about my principal.” That’s SIP. Dividend equities plus investment-grade fixed income, 35 to 65 in either direction, 50-to-70 names. The team adjusts the mix; the client gets the income.

02
SIP Plus — The Step-Up

“I want more yield but I still want someone watching the risk.” That’s SIP Plus. Same framework, but the fixed income sleeve adds selective BBB/BB-rated credit with a 50/50 target split and max 15% BB overweight. Plus a municipal bond option for tax-sensitive clients. Quality-first, not yield-chasing.

03
Global SIP — The Diversifier

“I want income, but I also want exposure beyond U.S. markets.” That’s Global SIP. ClariVest runs the international dividend equity sleeve; Eagle runs the domestic equity income and investment-grade bonds. One philosophy, broader opportunity set.
