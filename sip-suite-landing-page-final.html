The suite

Turn up the volume on your income strategy.

Each strategy draws from the same disciplined process while giving advisors a cleaner way to match client need to portfolio construction.

The anchor
Strategic Income Portfolio
Steady income and consistent growth

Combines dividend-paying equities and investment-grade fixed income in one actively managed framework for clients who want a more balanced income allocation.

Risk profileConservative to Moderate
Income levelModerate
Primary roleIncome anchor
Best positioned for:
  • Retirees prioritizing stability and capital discipline
  • Core income allocations
  • Clients who want a steady paycheck with growth potential
The step-up
SIP Plus
Higher income potential

Uses higher-yielding dividend equities alongside carefully constructed BBB/BB corporate credit to pursue more cash flow without losing the discipline of the broader suite framework.

Risk profileModerate to Moderate-High
Income levelHigh
Primary roleIncome step-up
Best positioned for:
  • Clients seeking higher cash flow
  • Tax-aware accounts needing municipal-bond flexibility
  • Income conversations where the current draw is not enough
The diversifier
Global SIP
Diversified, international income

Integrates international dividend equities with domestic investment-grade fixed income to widen the income opportunity set and reduce reliance on one market for income generation.

Risk profileModerate
Income levelModerate to High
Primary roleIncome diversification
Best positioned for:
  • Clients concentrated in domestic dividend exposure
  • Income portfolios that need geographic breadth
  • Conversations around valuation, dividends and global opportunity
