The Framework

One investment process.
Three calibrated solutions.

Eagle's tenured equity income and fixed income teams operate under a single tactical framework — each strategy calibrated to a distinct point on the income spectrum.

01

Active Tactical Management

Dynamic allocation adjustments based on macroeconomic indicators, relative value, and credit cycle positioning — not static targets.

02

Disciplined Construction

Bottom-up equity selection paired with rigorous fixed income analysis. Defined allocation ranges of 35-65% equities, 35-65% bonds, 0-30% cash.

03

Quality-First Income

Income generation that never compromises capital discipline. Dividend sustainability and credit quality are non-negotiable across all three strategies.
