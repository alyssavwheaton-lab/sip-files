Rates, Rotation & Income Positioning
March 2026: Rates, Rotation & Positioning3:42 • James Camp, CFA
Key takeaway: Credit spreads tightened meaningfully in February, creating selective opportunities in BB-rated corporates. The team maintained overweight positioning in quality high-yield while reducing duration exposure ahead of expected rate volatility.
Key takeaway: The team shifted equity emphasis toward companies with consistent dividend growth histories and strong free cash flow coverage, prioritizing sustainability of income over current yield in an environment where rate uncertainty may pressure lower-quality payers.
Key takeaway: 2025 delivered strong total returns across all three strategies. Looking ahead, the team sees a constructive backdrop for income investors, with moderating inflation supporting both equity dividends and fixed income positioning entering 2026.
